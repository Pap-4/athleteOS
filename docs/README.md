# AthleteOS - Problem Statement

## What problem does AthleteOS Solve

AthleteOS combines activites such as running, swimming, cycling, gym etc. with nutrition to form a centralised dashboard where athletes can log their workout, view sessions, and track their progress over time using visual charts. 

## Who is AthleteOS for
AthleteOS is targeted for the "active person" who will benefit from a diverse fitness dashboard, however it iis not limited to those who enjoy raising their heart rate and is accessible for all.
